A few quick answers

Good to know

How do I find restaurants that deliver to me?+

Enter your delivery location in the search box above. We’ll show nearby restaurants, current offers, and estimated delivery times for your area.

What is Neighborhood Drop?+

It’s a proposed opt-in shared delivery window for nearby orders. Orders remain individually packed and private. If a participating zone reaches its route threshold, eligible customers can receive a lower delivery fee. Availability and savings depend on location and timing.

Can I order groceries and restaurant food in one app?+

Yes. Explore restaurant delivery, groceries through Instamart, dining offers through Dineout, and local pick-up and drop with Genie, subject to availability in your area.
